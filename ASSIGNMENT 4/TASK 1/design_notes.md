# Task 1 – File-system design review
Recommended hierarchy: /students/<register-number>/{documents,submissions,projects,media}. Use unique institution-issued IDs, normalized filenames, metadata, quotas, ACLs and scheduled snapshots. Group work belongs in /projects/<course>/<team>, with explicit group ACLs and links/shortcuts from student directories. Use backup plus tested restore procedures. Cloud alternative: institutional Google Workspace/OneDrive with SSO, MFA, retention, audit logs and role-based sharing; evaluate provider lock-in, egress, data residency and administrative control.
