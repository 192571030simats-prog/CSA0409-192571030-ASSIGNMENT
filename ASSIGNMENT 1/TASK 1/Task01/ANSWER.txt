Requirements ranking: monitors—bounded alarm response under 10 ms, reliability, safety/security, sensor I/O, low memory/power; tablets—battery, security, wireless reliability, responsive UI; workstations—usability, privacy, network reliability; database—availability/integrity, security, throughput, recovery; MRI—parallel throughput, memory/storage bandwidth, reliability; partner link—confidentiality, availability, interoperability. Safety-critical: monitor alarms and accurate patient records. Batch history suits deferred reporting; time-sharing suits workstations; personal computing suits desktops; mobile OS design suits tablets. A general-purpose OS has no automatic hard worst-case deadline, usually needs more memory/storage, and may simplify administration but is unsuitable as a universal solution.
