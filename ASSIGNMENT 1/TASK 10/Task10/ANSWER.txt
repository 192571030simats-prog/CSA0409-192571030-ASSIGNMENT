Run the provided Linux script and benchmark. Use strace -c for syscall counts and /usr/bin/time -v for resource/time data. The benchmark creates and waits for 1,000 processes sequentially, then creates/joins 1,000 threads. Expected trend: larger buffers reduce read calls; threads are usually lighter because they share address space/resources, while processes are more isolated. Actual elapsed times depend on hardware, kernel and load; record genuine measurements and do not invent values.
