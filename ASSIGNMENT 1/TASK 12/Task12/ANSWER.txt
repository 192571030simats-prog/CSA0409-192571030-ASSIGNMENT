Decision: RETAIN real-time edge processing and core protection; MODIFY central records to a secure distributed/cloud service and extend management to home devices. Home monitors need local alarm decisions, timestamping, encrypted transmission, buffering during internet outages and safe retries; internet latency must not be part of a critical local alarm path. Cloud records need authenticated APIs, encryption at rest/in transit, role-based access, audit, replication, backups, disaster recovery and governance. Benefits are shared access and elastic capacity; trade-offs are recurring cost, internet dependence, privacy and integration complexity. Validate latency, fail-over, recovery objectives and security.
